Home  /  Our Story

In 1989, in a tiny back-room with nothing more than a telephone, a thick index of flights and a deep knowledge of the destination, husband-and-wife travellers Ashish and Jahnabi Phookan founded Jungle Travels India.

We were among the very first companies to introduce the world to East India's extraordinary biodiversity. More than three decades on, we cover wildlife and birding hotspots right across the country — but the Northeast remains our home and our heart.

Today JTI is part of JTI Group, a premier travel group operating award-winning brands across river cruises, boutique lodges and resorts, destination management, and textiles and handloom.
