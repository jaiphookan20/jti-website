intro:
- A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1985 and a Project Tiger reserve, Manas is spectacularly set where the plains of Assam meet the forested mountains of Bhutan. Fed by the Manas and Beki rivers, it is a mosaic of grassland, forest and shifting river channels — and a genuine conservation success story.
- 'The park''s northern range at Mathanguri offers one of the most beautiful views in the Northeast: the Manas river running out of the hills, and Bhutan''s forests rising beyond. We explore by jeep and by elephant, with time for the Bodo villages, tea gardens and weaving centres that ring the park.'
