intro:
- Nameri lies in the foothills of the Eastern Himalayas in Assam's Sonitpur district — excellent elephant country, with herds grazing the grass, and an ideal habitat for tigers, leopards, dhole, sloth bear and the capped langur.
- It is above all a birder's park, explored largely on foot in the forest. A raft down the Jia-Bhorelli river is the perfect way to add waterside species to the day's list.

birdline: Among its 300-plus species are the White-winged Wood Duck, Great Pied, Wreathed and Rufous-necked Hornbills, the Blue-bearded Bee-eater and the Ibisbill.
best: Best visited November to April, when the forest walking is at its finest and the river low enough for rafting.
reach: Near Tezpur in Sonitpur district, about 4 hours from Guwahati and easily paired with Kaziranga.
