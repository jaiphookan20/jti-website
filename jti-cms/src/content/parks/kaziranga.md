- 'A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1985, Kaziranga is Assam''s great wildlife stage: a vast mosaic of elephant grass, marshes and sal forest, threaded by the shifting channels of the Brahmaputra. It is a conservation success story written large — a rhino population brought back from the brink, thriving now alongside tiger, elephant, wild water buffalo and swamp deer, the so-called "Big Five" of Kaziranga.'
- We explore the park across its different ranges — Central (Kohora), Western (Bagori), Eastern (Agaratoli) and Burapahar — by jeep and by elephant, the early light burning off the mist as rhinos graze the grass. Between drives, there is time for the tea gardens and villages that ring the park.
