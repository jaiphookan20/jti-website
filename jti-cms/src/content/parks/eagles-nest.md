intro:
- Nestled in the Dafla Hills of the eastern Himalayas, Eagle's Nest covers 218 sq km and climbs from 500 to 3,500 metres — a range that packs in an astonishing diversity of habitat and species.
- It was here that the Bugun Liocichla was first noted in 1995 and formally described in 2006 by Ramana Athreya — one of the most celebrated ornithological discoveries of modern India. Days are spent birding the forest and camping in serviced tents.
