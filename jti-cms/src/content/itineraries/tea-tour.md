    title: Dibrugarh
    body: Following breakfast, you will visit the local tea gardens and factories to experience the tea manufacturing process firsthand. During the visit, you will have the opportunity to try tea leaf plucking and observe how the tea is transported to the factory. You will view the entire production cycle, including withering, the Crushing, Tearing, and Curling (CTC) process, drying, and packaging. Staying overnight at the same tea bungalow.

  - label: Day 3
    title: Dibrugarh – Sibsagar – Jorhat (150 km/3 hrs)
    body: After breakfast drive to Jorhat. visit Sibsagar, which also served as the capital of the Ahom kings. Sibsagar serves as a testament to the Ahoms' enduring legacy and the unique blend of Indian and Southeast Asian influences that shaped their culture. Shan by origin but later converted to Hinduism, the Ahoms ruled Assam for some 600 years until the mid-1820s. One of their many architectural feats was recently awarded the UNESCO World Heritage tag. Following the day's activities, you will drive to Jorhat to stay at a golf resort situated within a tea estate. The property overlooks a golf course, where you will spend two nights.
    stay: ''
    photo: /assets/uploads/temple_shikhara_HD.jpg
  - label: Day 4
    title: Jorhat
    body: AM visit the Tocklai Tea Research Station where you can have an insight on the latest trends of tea plantation and manufacture, see miniature tea machinery and also a tea museum. It is worth mentioning that Tocklai Tea Experimental Station is one of the leading tea research stations in the world and is funded by the Government of India and Indian Tea Association. (closed on Saturdays and Sundays) After lunch go for a short visit of the Gymkhana Club near Jorhat town. The Jorhat Gymkhana Club is one of the oldest institutions in India and has an impressive history. The Jorhat Gymkhana Club boasts of an impressive accomplishment of having the world’s third oldest golf course. It has an 18-hole golf course and a unique tradition of an annual pony race, popularly known as the Jorhat Race. The first plane ever in the North-East landed in its grounds in 1928, and the first jeep in Assam was also displayed at the club’s ground. Stay another night at the golf resort.

    photo: /assets/uploads/m_Jorhat_Gymkhana_Club_1_l_427_640.webp
  - label: Day 5
    title: Jorhat – Kaziranga National Park (100 km/ 2 ½ hrs)
    body: After breakfast transfer to Kaziranga National Park, known for the world’s largest population of Indian One Horned Rhinoceros. On arrival check into hotel. Evening at leisure. Overnight. The UNESCO World Heritage site, Kaziranga National Park hosts two-thirds of the world’s Great One-horned rhinoceros also known for the highest density of tigers among the protected areas in the world and of course swamp deer (barasingha), Elephant and wild water buffalos too. Bordered by Brahmaputra River on the north and Karbi hills on the south, this nature reserve is one of the finest national parks in the country. Apart from the mammals, this park is also home to a variety of migratory birds, water birds, predators, scavengers, and game birds.

  - label: Day 7
    title: Kaziranga National Park – Guwahati (220 kms/5 hrs)
    body: Today morning drive to Guwahati and check into a hotel. Later visit the famous Kamakhya temple, situated on top of Nilachal hill. This temple is revered as one of the most sacred and ancient 51 Shakti Peethas in the Indian subcontinent and is devoted to the eternal feminine, the goddess of desire, “Kamakhya” – granter of desires. It symbolizes the "fusion of faiths and practices" of Aryan and non-Aryan elements in Assam. Overnight.

  - label: Day 8
    title: Guwahati
    body: Visit Guwahati Tea Auction Center after breakfast. This is the largest auction center for CTC tea in the world. Watch buyers bid for teas and marvel at the frantic pace of large quantities of tea being auctioned. From here we take you to a tea broking House where you can see tea being tasted and valued. Nearly 200 cups of tea are tasted daily. Experts in the tea line will explain all your queries, if you have any. (Tea Auction Center is open on Tuesdays and Wednesdays only) Evening take an exclusive 1-hour cruise in River Brahmaputra by a motorized country boat.  Rest of the evening at leisure. Overnight.

  - label: Day 12
    title: Kolkata
    body: In the morning, visit Flurys Cafe and enjoy an English breakfast. After breakfast, stroll through the colorful flower market (Mullick Ghat) under the Howrah Bridge. Then, visit the Maiden Garden. There, admire St. Paul's Cathedral, the National Museum, and Fort William. A highlight is the Victoria Memorial, built of white marble in 1906 in honor of Queen Victoria. Pass by the towers of the High Court and Raj Bhawan, then head to BBD Bagh (Dalhousie Square), where you will visit the Writers' Building, the General Post Office, and St. John's Church, built in 1787.
