intro: "This eight-day journey threads together the finest of Assam's protected wild: Manas, spectacularly set against the mountains of Bhutan; Nameri, a birder's forest on the Jia-Bhorelli river; and Kaziranga, home to two-thirds of the world's one-horned rhinos. You'll travel by jeep and by elephant, on foot and by raft, with our own naturalists reading the forest for you — and, between safaris, time among the tea gardens, weavers and Bodo villages that make this landscape human as well as wild."

days:
  - label: Day 1
    title: Arrive Guwahati
    body: Arrive at Guwahati and settle into your hotel. Your first taste of Northeast birding begins nearby at Deepor Beel Wildlife Sanctuary — a Ramsar wetland alive with waterfowl on the city's edge.
    stay: Guwahati
    photo: ''
  - label: Days 2–3
    title: Guwahati → Manas National Park
    body: Drive to Manas — a Project Tiger reserve and World Heritage Site, spectacularly located in the Himalayan foothills. Explore over two days on jeep and elephant safari, watching for tiger, rhino, golden langur and the elusive pygmy hog. There's time, too, for a nearby Bodo village, a tea garden and a local weaving centre.
    stay: Lodge outside Manas National Park
    photo: /assets/uploads/black-leopard-safari-in-india.jpg
  - label: Days 4–5
    title: Manas → Nameri National Park
    body: Drive on to Nameri, exploring the forest largely on foot in pursuit of its rich birdlife. There's the option to raft the Jia-Bhorelli river, drifting past waterside birds — Ibisbill, mergansers and more — as the current carries you.
    stay: Eco Camp, outside Nameri National Park
    photo: /assets/uploads/NNP Rafting2.jpeg
  - label: Days 6–7
    title: Nameri → Kaziranga National Park
    body: Continue to Kaziranga, jewel of Assam, exploring its different ranges by jeep and elephant. Between safaris, opt to wander nearby villages and tea estates. This is the great stage of the one-horned rhino, alongside tiger, wild water buffalo and swamp deer.
    stay: Diphlu River Lodge or similar
    photo: /assets/uploads/OHR.jfif
  - label: Day 8
    title: Kaziranga → Guwahati → Onward
    body: Transfer to Guwahati airport for your onward journey, the Northeast's wild abundance behind you.
