    title: ''
    body: Arrive to embark your private and exclusive riverboat by 09:00 AM. The vessel will be waiting for you near Pandu Ghat, Guwahati and our Manager and his team will meet you with a welcome drink. We shall start cruising downstream towards the little town of Sualkuchi, visit its silk weaving workshops on e-rickshaw to see the whole process of silk manufacture, from cocoon to spinning to dyeing and finally to hand weaving into exquisite saris and dress lengths. Sualkuchi is also known as the textile center of Assam, Sualkuchi is famous for its high-quality Muga, Pat and Eri silk products. Local attires like ‘Mekhela Chador’, ‘Sari’ and ‘Gamosa’ are made from these indigenous silk materials which have a great demand throughout Assam and India. After exploring Sualkuchi, return to your vessel and enjoy your lunch while cruising downstream, moored close to the village of Bijoynagar. Explore this small Assamese town on your bicycles before returning to the vessel.
