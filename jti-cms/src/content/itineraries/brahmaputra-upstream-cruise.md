    photo: /assets/uploads/Assamese lunch served on M.V. Rudra Sing.jpg
  - label: Day 3
    title: ''
    body: After an early breakfast, drive for around two hours to the Nameri National Park at the foot of the blue hills of Himalaya. On arrival at Nameri National Park drive for another 30 minutes and take a float trip of around two hours down the Jia-Bhoreli River through the park. There are excellent bird-watching opportunities here (look out for the great Hornbills) and, perhaps, the chance of sighting wild Elephants. Lunch will be served outside. Post lunch trek inside the park and look for birds like Merganser, Pallas’s fish eagle, lapwings, cormorants, and ruddy shell duck, kingfishers and perhaps the elusive White-Winged Duck or the Great Indian Hornbill. After all the activities, return to the vessel by dusk. Dinner will be served onboard.
    stay: ''

  - label: Day 4
    title: ''
    body: This morning, start sailing towards Silghat. Upon arrival, disembark and drive for about an hour to reach Diphlu River Lodge in Kaziranga National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In the afternoon, enjoy a jeep safari inside the National Park. In the evening, enjoy a cup of tea while local artisans perform naam-kirton (a form of prayer) by the lodge pond.
