  title: Pelling – Kalimpong
  body: After breakfast check out and transfer to Kalimpong (4100ft., 95Kms/5hrs appx), It’s a quiet hill station in eastern Himalaya. Kalimpong is famous for its Orchids & Nurseries and some of the some of the rarest cacti & Orchids can be seen. Oak, Maple and chestnut stretch up the ridge as far as the eye can reach. Check in at hotel. Overnight at hotel.

  title: Kalimpong sightseeing & transfer to Darjeeling
  body: After breakfast check out and start for local sightseeing in and around Kalimpong City, covering Durpin Dara monastery (Zong Dog Palri Fo-Brong Monastery), Flower & Orchid Nurseries, Dr. Graham’s Home, Dello Hills & Kalimpong Market. Later transfer to Darjeeling (7000ft, 55kms/3hrs appx.). It is the place of Dorje, the mystic thunderbolt of the Lama-Religion was the origin of the name Darjeeling, which was first discovered by a British Captain Lloyd in the year 1829. It will cater you a mix-experience of lively nature & charming Himalayan people. Surrounded by Tea gardens, this little town faces some of the Himalayas highest peaks. Check in at hotel. Evening is free for individual activities and leisure. Overnight at Darjeeling.

- label: Day 12
  title: Excursion to North Bay & Ross Islands
  body: After breakfast set out for a day excursion tour to North Bay also known as the "Coral Island" of the Andaman. It is the premier hub for water sports and is renowned for its vibrant fringing coral reefs, underwater marine life, and an iconic lighthouse featured on the Indian Rs. 20 note. Also visit Ross Island (closed on Wednesday) - once the administrative headquarters of the British, it is famous for its dense greenery, roaming deer, and sprawling colonial ruins. Afternoon return to Port Blair.

  photo: ''
- label: Day 13
  title: Port Blair – Havelock island (Swaraj Dweep)
  body: After breakfast check out and embark on the vessel for cruise to Havelock Island, the cruise departs at (6:45 am to01:30 pm any time in between). So we have to report an hour early. Take a quick breakfast and head to Havelock Island the total journey time is around 1 and 1/2 hour by sea. On arrival transfer to hotel. Afternoon take a 40 minutes’ cruise to the Elephant beach – embark on the most memorable snorkeling trip, this beach offers snorkelers calm blue shallow waters and is the perfect spot for beginner snorkelers. The reef here starts very close to shore at a depth of about 1 meter. The large reef is mostly made up of hard coral and has plenty of marine life Afternoon visit the Radhanagar beach. Overnight in Havelock. (Boat transfers are subjected to change as per the availability of tickets and weather conditions).
